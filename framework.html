Foundation

What is TM Forum SID — and why does it matter?

SID is the telco industry's agreed model for what business concepts exist and how they relate. Every major telco — Telstra, Verizon, Deutsche Telekom, Singtel — uses SID as the reference. It is not a database schema. It is a map of business meaning.

SID organises telco concepts into Subject Business Areas

Each Subject Business Area (SBA) groups related business entities together. The logic is simple: if you can draw a boundary around a set of concepts that a single business function owns, that is an SBA. Each SBA becomes a data domain. Each entity within it becomes a candidate data product.

📐

The derivation logic

Each SID entity that has operational data, is owned by a specific domain team, and is consumed by other domains or AI agents becomes a data product. Not every entity needs its own product — related entities are grouped where they are always consumed together.

🔗

Why not just look at Purview?

Purview shows you what data assets exist today — tables, files, views. SID shows you what data products should exist based on how a telco operates. The gap between the two is your data product roadmap. SID gives you the target state; Purview gives you the current state.

✅

Telstra already uses TM Forum

Telstra is a TM Forum member and has historically used SID as a reference model for its BSS/OSS architecture. Your governance ontology already aligns to TM Forum SID for the Party entity. This is not a new standard — it is the existing standard applied to the data product layer.

Prioritisation

How to decide which data products to build first

Not all 35+ data products are equal priority. Three questions determine the build sequence.

Question 1 — Highest Priority
Does Kim's autonomous network fail without it?
Network Performance, Network Topology, SLA Performance, Customer Usage, Customer Value Tier, Service Instance
→ Build now. Critical path.
Question 2 — Non-negotiable
Does a regulatory obligation require it?
Consent & Privacy, Regulatory Reporting, Fraud Detection — required by Privacy Act, ACMA, and financial regulation regardless of strategy
→ Build in parallel. No choice.
Question 3 — Commercial
Does it directly protect or grow revenue?
Churn Signal, Customer Experience, Contract & Agreement, Fraud Detection — revenue protection and growth levers
→ Build next. High ROI.
Recommended Approach

How to use this framework with domain teams

Rather than starting from a blank page, take this SID + ODA derived list into domain team conversations as a validated hypothesis. The conversation becomes a validation exercise, not an invention exercise.

Done — POC
12 synthetic data products in governance graph
Customer-360, Service-NBN, Billing-Summary and 9 others are already modelled, governed, and queryable at waranara.com/agent. SID-aligned from the start.
Step 1 — Now
Domain roadmap conversations
Go to each domain team with this SID + ODA derived list. Ask: does this match your roadmap? What's in flight? What's missing? What's the wrong priority?
Step 2 — Per domain
Draft core glossary terms per planned product
For each planned product, draft 2-3 core business concepts. Not validated yet — working drafts. Load as GlossaryTerm nodes marked "draft" in the governance graph.
Step 3 — At In-Review stage
EIO validation — 15 minutes per product
When a product reaches In-Review maturity, the relevant EIO validates the glossary terms. Not a definition exercise — a confirmation that the terms match how their function thinks about the data.
Step 4 — Published
Agents can navigate the data landscape autonomously
Published, governed data products with validated glossary terms are findable by AI agents without human direction. Kim's agentic systems have trusted data to work from.
